# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code applies to repository Issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions,
and other public collaboration around this project.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- communicate professionally and respectfully;
- focus criticism on claims, evidence, code, methods, and repository decisions rather than individuals;
- distinguish observed facts, platform responses, inference, proposals, and unknown state;
- correct material errors openly and preserve provenance when historical records must remain auditable;
- respect privacy, security boundaries, attribution, copyright, and third-party licenses;
- avoid spam, harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, fabricated evidence, or knowingly misleading validation claims.

### 科研诚信与证据状态 / Research integrity and evidence state

公开协作要区分真实原始观测、诊断采集、推断、静态文档检查与已执行回放，准确保留未执行、部分完成、失败、限流和不可核实状态；发现错误通过显式更正或有时间戳的后续记录处理，不伪造成功或倒改冻结证据

Public collaboration distinguishes raw eligible observations, diagnostics, inference, static document checks and actual replays. Keep NOT_EXECUTED, PARTIAL, FAILED, rate-limited and unresolved status visible. Correct mistakes through explicit dated reconciliation rather than fabricating success or rewriting frozen evidence

## Research and engineering integrity

Do not present an unexecuted check as passed, an unobserved state as observed,
or a later live state as historical proof.

Unknown, blocked, rate-limited, partial, degraded, failed, and no-conclusion
states are legitimate research outcomes and must not be hidden merely to make a
record appear complete.

## Sensitive information

Do not publish credentials, private communications, access tokens, cookies,
private or expiring share links, personal data unrelated to the research
question, or unpublished exploit details.

Follow `SECURITY.md` for security-sensitive reports.

## Moderation

The repository maintainer may edit, hide, remove, lock, or reject contributions
that violate these standards or materially undermine repository integrity.

The repository owner retains final moderation and merge authority.
