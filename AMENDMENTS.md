# Amendments

Append-only ledger for substantive post-registration changes to study procedure.

No amendments are recorded by the bootstrap commit.

## Entry template

### AMEND-YYYYMMDD-NN — Short title

- **Recorded:** ISO-8601 timestamp
- **Effective:** ISO-8601 timestamp or event condition
- **Scope:** affected contract/schedule/schema/analysis component
- **Reason:** why the change is necessary
- **Original rule:** prior rule
- **Amended rule:** new rule
- **Expected effect:** likely effect on observations or interpretation
- **Preregistration status:** post-registration amendment
- **Related evidence/PR/commit:** references

Entries must not rewrite or delete older entries.
