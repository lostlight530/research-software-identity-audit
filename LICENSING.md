tooling, workflows, and documentation are distributed under the
[MIT License](./LICENSE).

The SPDX identifier is `MIT`.

## External evidence and third-party material

Evidence collection does not transfer ownership or relicense third-party
material.

Externally sourced records, quoted material, archived responses, screenshots,
metadata exports, and other third-party artifacts retain their original
attribution, copyright, license, database-rights, and platform terms where
applicable.

Only the minimum evidence needed to support a research claim should be retained.

## Separate scholarly objects

The OSF preregistration is a separate scholarly object and is released under
CC BY 4.0 on OSF. Its license does not replace the repository's MIT license.

A Zenodo archive of this repository may describe the repository release as MIT.
That archive metadata does not grant new rights over third-party evidence
included only under its original source terms.

## Contributions

Contributions to repository-owned material are submitted under the same MIT
terms unless an explicit contribution states otherwise and is accepted by the
maintainer.
