tooling, workflows, and documentation are distributed under the
[MIT License](./LICENSE).

The SPDX identifier is `MIT`.

## External evidence and third-party material

Evidence collection does not transfer ownership or relicense third-party
material.

Externally sourced records, quoted material, archived responses, screenshots,
metadata exports, and other third-party artifacts retain their original
attribution, copyright, license, database-rights, and platform terms where
applicable.

Only the minimum evidence needed to support a research claim should be retained.
